Privacy policy

Stashew · last updated 17 October 2026

Stashew is a personal expense manager for Android and macOS, built by an individual developer for personal use. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does with it.

The short version

Your data stays on your devices and in your own Google Drive, encrypted. The developer never receives it. Nothing is shared with anyone.

Data the app stores

The financial information you enter or import: accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals and settings. It is stored in a database on each device where you use Stashew.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google to sync your devices, Stashew requests:

  • Your basic Google account details (name, email address), to show which account is connected.
  • Access to the files Stashew creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). Stashew creates a Stashew folder and keeps its sync files and backups there. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Everything Stashew writes to your Drive is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know, before it is uploaded.

This data is used only to sync your own devices and keep backups. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train any model.

Stashew's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Stashew does not do

  • It has no server and no Stashew account.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It never connects to your bank: it only reads export files you choose to import.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data stays on a device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • To remove the synced copy, delete the Stashew folder from your Google Drive.
  • To withdraw Stashew's access to your Google account, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked by opening an issue in this website's repository.
