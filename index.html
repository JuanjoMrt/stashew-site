Stashew

A personal expense manager for Android and macOS.

Stashew keeps track of one person's accounts, expenses, budgets and goals. It imports the bank's own exports instead of relying on typing every expense, reconciles them against the bank, and helps understand where the money goes.

It is a personal project, built for its author's own use. It is not published on any app store and is not offered to other users.

How it handles data

  • Everything is stored on the user's own devices.
  • To keep the phone and the computer in sync, Stashew uses a folder in the user's own Google Drive, and can only access the files it created there. Everything it writes there is encrypted on the device first.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no server, no account with Stashew, no analytics and no advertising.

Details are in the privacy policy.

Stashew is not affiliated with Google, nor with any bank.
